      Frequently Asked Questions

      Is it really safe to upload my bank statements here?

      Yes, because you aren't actually "uploading" anything. All the code that powers this PDF tool is downloaded to your browser the moment you visit the page. When you select a file, the processing happens directly on your own computer's memory. No data is transmitted over the internet.

      Why didn't my PDF compress very much?

      Because this tool runs in the browser, it focuses on structural compression—removing invisible bloat, deleted objects, and excess metadata. If your PDF is massive because it contains dozens of high-resolution scanned photographs, true compression would require downsampling those images, which is difficult to perform efficiently in a client-side environment.
