      Client-Side Document Architecture

      Managing digital documents shouldn't compromise your privacy. Our PDF Suite offers secure document manipulation tools that run directly inside your web browser. This means your sensitive tax documents, legal forms, and personal files are processed locally on your device and never uploaded to an external server.

      Core Capabilities

      • Combine & Merge: Easily stitch together multiple PDF documents into a single file. Perfect for creating consolidated reports or portfolios.
      • Split & Extract: Pull out specific pages from massive documents by simply entering the page ranges you need.
      • Image Conversion: Convert JPGs and PNGs into standardized A4 or Letter-sized documents, complete with custom margins and orientation settings.
      • Local Optimization: Our compressor re-indexes your document and safely strips out unused metadata, reducing the overall file size without sacrificing text quality.

      Frequently Asked Questions

      Is it safe to upload secure forms here?

      You aren't actually "uploading" anything. All the code that powers this PDF tool is downloaded to your browser the moment you visit the page. When you select a file, the processing happens directly on your own computer's memory. No data is transmitted over the internet.

      Why didn't my PDF compress very much?

      Because this tool runs in the browser, it focuses on structural compression—removing invisible bloat, deleted objects, and excess metadata. If your PDF is massive because it contains dozens of high-resolution scanned photographs, true compression would require downsampling those images, which is difficult to perform efficiently in a strictly client-side environment.
